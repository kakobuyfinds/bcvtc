What to check before ordering

Match the size and color

Open the seller listing and confirm the selected variant. For clothing, compare the seller’s measurements with a garment you already own. For shoes, check how the seller measures foot or insole length.

Review QC photos

If warehouse photos are available, compare the size label, color and visible stitching with the listing. Ask for close-ups if a measurement or detail is missing, and check the return window before approving shipment.

Estimate the total cost

Add the item price, seller-to-warehouse shipping, any agent fees and the international shipping quote. Check whether the carrier charges by actual or volumetric weight and whether taxes are due at delivery.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

How do I find an item from the table?

Search HipoBuyFind by product name and match the item ID in the result. Use the original seller URL from the product page to check whether OrientDig accepts the listing.

Why did the price change after I selected a size or color?

A seller can set different prices for different variants. Use the amount shown for your selected variant when estimating the order total.

Can I use a QC photo from another listing?

Use it as a visual reference. To evaluate your own order, check photos of the exact item received at the warehouse, if available.
